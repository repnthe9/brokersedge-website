A checklist of costs to ask about

  • Setup and implementation fees. Some vendors charge a one-time fee on top of the monthly price.
  • Seats and logins. Per-user and per-login pricing grows as your team does.
  • Load or shipment caps. Ask what the overage rate is and whether it is published.
  • Integration fees. Load boards, factoring, tracking and accounting connections can be extra.
  • Contract length. Ask about renewal terms and cancellation.
  • Trial. Can you run real loads before you commit?
