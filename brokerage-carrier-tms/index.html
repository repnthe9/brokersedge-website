The problem with two systems

When brokered freight lives in one tool and your own trucks live in another, you re-key loads, reconcile two sets of books and never see true margin. BrokersEdge puts it all in one place.

One load board

Book a load once, then cover it with an outside carrier, an owner-operator or one of your own trucks. Same statuses, same documents, same tracking.

One dispatch view

See every truck you control and every carrier you have booked, with live location from your driver app, ELDs like Samsara and Motive, or MacroPoint and TruckerTools.

One set of books

Customer receivables, carrier payables and driver pay share the same ledger, so margin by load is the real number.

Driver app included

Drivers get a mobile app with background GPS, document upload and status updates. No separate fleet product to buy.

Carrier-friendly payments
