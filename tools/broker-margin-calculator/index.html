How to read the result

Margin vs markup

Margin is profit divided by what you bill. Markup is profit divided by what you pay the carrier. Most brokers quote in margin, so compare that number first.

Count your TMS per load

A tool that charges per seat gets more expensive as you add dispatchers, even if your load count stays flat. Cost per load shows what it takes out of each deal.
