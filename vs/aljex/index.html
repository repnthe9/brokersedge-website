Where Aljex is stronger

Worth knowing before you decide.

Unlimited shipments

If you move very high volume, a flat per-user plan with no load cap can cost less than a per-load overage model.

Long track record and EDI depth

Aljex has been serving brokerages for years, is part of Descartes, and includes customer EDI connections on higher plans.

Hands-on rollout

Higher plans include hours of training, go-live monitoring and a dedicated success manager.
