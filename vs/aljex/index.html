Questions to ask Aljex (or any TMS vendor)

Use these to compare any system against what BrokersEdge includes.

  • How long until we are live, and is there a free trial we can use on real loads?
  • What does the price include? Are features split into tiers or add-ons?
  • Can customers open a live tracking link with map and ETA, or do we send updates by hand?
  • How are factored carriers paid? Is remittance and ACH built in?
  • Can we receive tenders by API or EDI 204 and answer with 990 and 214?
  • Can we dispatch our own trucks and outside carriers from the same board?
