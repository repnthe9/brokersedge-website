FAQ

Questions brokers ask

Can I quote and dispatch LTL too?

Yes. BrokersEdge is built around truckload and regular freight, and you can also quote and dispatch LTL from the same board.

How does factoring work for carriers?

Link a carrier to its factoring company once. Pay runs then use the factor's remit-to emails (you can add several), and factored loads skip the carrier due-date countdown.

Can customers track without an account?

Yes. You send a tracking link; they see status, last location update, speed and a live ETA with no login.

Can I run my own trucks and brokered freight together?

Yes. Outside carriers, owner-operators and company trucks share one board and one set of books.
