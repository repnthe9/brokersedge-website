FAQ

Questions new brokers ask

Do I need a credit card to try it?

No. The 30-day trial is free with full access to every feature, and you can cancel anytime.

I'm just starting out. Is it too much for me?

No. The Starter plan covers 50 loads a month for $150, with every feature included. Extra loads are $3 each, and you move up only when you grow.

I already have loads and carriers in another system. Do I have to re-enter them?

No. Send us your carriers, customers and open loads in a spreadsheet or export and we load them in for you.

Can customers track a load without an account?

Yes. You send a tracking link and they see status, last location update, speed and a live ETA with no login.

How does factoring work for carriers?

Link a carrier to its factoring company once. Pay runs then use the factor's remit-to emails, and factored loads skip the carrier due-date countdown.

Can I run my own trucks and brokered freight together?

Yes. Outside carriers, owner-operators and company trucks share one board and one set of books. You can also quote and dispatch LTL from the same board.
